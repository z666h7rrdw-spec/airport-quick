Airport Quick Guide — United States (50 States)
FAA NASR effective 2026-09-03

Includes 4,700 open public-use airports in all 50 U.S. states.
Source: FAA NASR CSV subscriber files supplied by user.

Features:
- Search by ICAO/FAA identifier, airport name, or city
- Runway length/width, surface, lighting, and traffic direction
- Selected weather/CTAF/UNICOM or towered-airport ATC frequencies
- Manual offline runway-wind calculator with visual wind arrows
- Correct parsing for runway designators such as 05L/23R
- Offline PWA after first successful load

Operational note: This is a quick-reference aid, not a substitute for current FAA publications, NOTAMs, ATIS/AWOS/ASOS, or required preflight information.
